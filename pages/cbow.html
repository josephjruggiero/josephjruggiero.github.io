Joseph J. Ruggiero

Assistant professor at UVA

Crisis Bargaining Over What?


Using a game-free approach, I study crisis bargaining games with uncertainty about the common value of the contested object, or stakes. I show that, under mild conditions, there is no crisis bargaining game with uncertain stakes in which transfers can remedy a bargaining failure caused by an indivisibility of the object. The obstacle is that uncertainty about stakes breaks the equivalence between allocating shares of the object and exchanging transfers, such as side payments or concessions on other issues. This is not true for the standard sources of uncertainty about power and the costs of war, which preserve that equivalence. Whether states are unable to reach an agreement or maintain it depends on the location of the indivisibility. I also show that correlated information can exacerbate the problem by amplifying demands when stakes are thought to be high.
